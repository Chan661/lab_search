# Lab-First Pipeline

Use this pipeline to discover active UC San Diego faculty, labs, research groups, and projects that may support advertised or unadvertised undergraduate opportunities.

## Source Order

Start with Computer Science and Engineering, HDSI/data science, artificial intelligence, and bioinformatics or computational biology. Expand selectively to ECE, Bioengineering, Jacobs School sources, and other qualifying UCSD units only when work is substantially computational or preferred sources yield too few viable candidates.

Follow the priorities, domain tiers, enabled state, and instructions in `search/sources/source_catalog.yml`.

## Discovery Progression

Move through `research area → faculty/lab/group → current activity → relevant project → undergraduate pathway → opening or contact route`. Do not skip from a directory entry to a recruitment claim.

## Query Strategy

- Use `broad_academic_discovery` to find research structures.
- Use `lab_and_project_discovery` to find groups, projects, and undergraduate pathways.
- Use `technical_specialization` to refine relevance.
- Use `advertised_roles` only when looking for explicit openings.

Start broadly enough to discover research that is not described using employment terminology.

## Verify Each Prospect

Check current UCSD appointment or qualifying affiliation, current lab activity, relevant research, undergraduate participation, availability, application or contact route, eligibility, compensation or credit, target-period compatibility, and verification date where available. Prefer current authoritative sources.

## Classify Recruitment Status

Apply the project definitions for `Open`, `Potential`, `Unclear`, `Closed`, `Stale`, and `Ineligible`. An active lab, recent publication, funded project, or history of undergraduate participation does not independently prove current recruitment.

## Decide Whether a Prospect Becomes a Candidate

Require current evidence of qualifying UCSD affiliation, meaningful relevance, a plausible undergraduate pathway, and a useful next action. That action may be to apply officially, verify eligibility, review a current project, identify the appropriate recipient, prepare outreach in a later stage, or monitor for an opening.

Keep a research structure without a plausible pathway or useful next action as a discovery note rather than an opportunity candidate.

## Stop or Expand

Do not weaken evidence standards to reach a quota. Stop or report incomplete coverage when selected sources have been processed, the bounded workload has been reached, expansion is unlikely to improve relevance, access prevents verification, or the user must select between materially different expansion paths. Record unsearched sources and research areas.
