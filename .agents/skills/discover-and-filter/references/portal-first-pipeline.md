# Portal-First Pipeline

Use this pipeline for UCSD REAL, Handshake, and user-supplied portal listings.

## Search REAL

Use the REAL source record and apply broad academic, lab/project, advertised-role, and technical-specialization profiles. Include advertised research positions, undergraduate projects, internships, volunteer research, academic-credit opportunities, and relevant technical or analytical roles. Do not restrict discovery to conventional employment titles.

## Search Handshake

Require the on-campus filter unless the user explicitly changes institutional scope. Apply broad academic, advertised-role, technical-specialization, and portal-specific terms from the source catalog. Exclude external employers unless a qualifying UCSD affiliation is independently verified.

## Inspect Full Listings

Do not evaluate from cards or snippets alone. Capture official identity, organization, responsibilities, status, eligibility, deadline, target period, compensation or credit, work arrangement, application or contact route, and verification date where available. Portal presence alone does not establish institutional affiliation.

## Handle Authentication and Access

Never request, store, or reproduce credentials, session cookies, MFA codes, or secrets.

If authenticated browser access is available, ask the user to complete login when necessary, use only the active session, expect user participation for MFA or CAPTCHA, and do not modify the external account.

If authenticated access is unavailable, process supplied listing content when provided, mark the portal and coverage incomplete, record a resume step, and continue accessible work. Do not claim that the portal was fully searched. An authentication failure in one portal does not invalidate verified candidates from other sources.

## Check Status and Freshness

Verify that a listing is current before assigning `Open`. Mark expired or explicitly unavailable listings `Closed`. Mark listings with questionable dates or inaccessible central details `Stale` or `Unclear`, as supported by evidence. Record the inspection date.

## Decide Whether a Listing Advances

Require verified institutional scope, meaningful responsibilities, no verified hard ineligibility, current or actionable status, and enough evidence for secondary evaluation. Preserve unknown compensation and unmentioned international-student eligibility without treating either as an automatic exclusion.
